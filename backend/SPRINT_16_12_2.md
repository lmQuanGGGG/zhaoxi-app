# Sprint 16.12.2 — Trusted Device Identity & Profile Persistence

Cumulative hotfix built on green/ready Sprint 16.12.1. First QR creates a temporary guest identity. The scanning device receives a ZhaoXi trusted-device credential. Saving meaningful profile information promotes the guest to a persistent account. The same scanner device can then identify the same account on future QR logins. Unsaved guests expire server-side.
