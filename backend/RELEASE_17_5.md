# ZhaoXi 17.5 — Unified Operations Command Center, Workload Planning, SLA Forecast & Management Dashboard

Baseline: uploaded ZhaoXi 17.4 GREEN + READY.

## Unified command center
Aggregates operational backlog from:
- Admin Support Desk
- Customer Operations Tasks
- Service Recovery
- Housing leads/viewing workload
- Travel booking/appointment workload

## Management dashboard
- Total backlog
- Overdue
- Due within 2 hours
- Due within 24 hours
- Due today
- Unassigned workload
- Critical service recovery
- Overloaded agents
- Module workload distribution
- Bottleneck indicators

## SLA forecast
Forecast buckets:
- already overdue
- next 2 hours
- next 24 hours
- next 72 hours
- management risk: healthy / watch / high / critical

## Workload planning
Admin capacity settings:
- daily capacity
- warning load threshold
- critical load threshold
- availability
Capacity changes are planning metadata only and do not automatically assign work.

## Guardrails
- no automatic assignment
- no automatic workflow mutation
- no automatic customer contact
- no automatic financial actions
- no Partner funds authority
- no payment routing / refund / settlement authority
