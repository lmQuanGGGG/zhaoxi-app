# Sprint 16.31 — Restaurant Analytics, Revenue & Promotion Performance

Cumulative release on Backend 16.30.1 and Platform 16.30.

## Partner analytics
- Dedicated `/analytics` workspace for Partner.
- Period filters: 7 / 30 / 90 days.
- Metrics are restricted to the authenticated Partner organization.
- Food analytics only count external-food orders created by the restaurant workflow.

## Revenue metrics
For completed food orders, Backend aggregates:
- Customer GMV / final order value.
- Base item revenue before item promotion.
- Item-promotion discount.
- Coupon discount.
- Net food revenue after item promotion and coupon.
- Gross delivery fee.
- Restaurant delivery subsidy.
- Customer-paid delivery fee.
- Average order value.

## Order metrics
- Total food orders.
- Completed orders.
- Cancelled/rejected orders.
- In-progress orders.
- Completion rate.
- Cancellation rate.
- Average preparation time using preparation start -> food ready timestamps when available.

## Daily trend
- Daily orders.
- Daily completed orders.
- Daily GMV.
- Daily net food revenue.
- Daily promotion discount.
- Daily coupon discount.
- Daily delivery subsidy.

## Product performance
- Top menu items by completed revenue.
- Quantity sold.
- Number of completed orders containing the item.
- Item-level promotion discount.

## Coupon/campaign performance
- Lifetime coupon used count.
- Period redemptions.
- Completed orders using each coupon.
- Completed revenue generated by coupon orders.
- Completed coupon discount.
- Redemption discount during the selected period.

## Compatibility
- No database migration is required.
- Coupon/redemption control from 16.30 remains authoritative.
- Promotion pricing from 16.29 remains authoritative.
- Restaurant Operations, Kitchen Queue and External Delivery remain unchanged.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
