# Sprint 16.61 — Partner Trust Governance, Manual Certification & Verification Badges

Built cumulatively on 16.60 GREEN/READY.

Admin can manually issue and revoke four verification badges: Verified Partner, High Service Quality, Reliable Support, Quality Recovered. Every issuance requires a reason and can include evidence reference and expiry date. Revocation requires a reason. Superseded, expired and revoked records remain in certification history.

Trust Score never auto-grants a badge. Certification is an explicit manual governance decision with audit logging.

Partner sees active badges and full certification history.

Badges are operational verification only. They do not automatically alter search ranking, visibility, platform fees, payment routing, settlement, refunds or access to Partner funds.

No database migration. Single-language mandatory.
