# Sprint 16.60 — Partner Trust Center, Compliance History & Operational Reputation

Cumulative on 16.59 GREEN/READY.

Trust Center consolidates Payment Support Quality Score, current risk, SLA/refund performance, compliance cases, interventions and recovery history into one operational Partner reputation profile.

Trust Score is 0–100 and starts from Quality Score, applies transparent risk/active-case penalties, and recognizes successful compliance recovery. Tiers: excellent, trusted, standard, watch, restricted_review.

Admin sees platform Trust Center, aggregate trust health, Partner ranking and compliance/recovery history.
Partner sees its own Trust Profile, performance and recovery history.

Trust is operational reputation only. It is not a customer-facing star rating and does not automatically alter ranking, visibility, fees, gateway routing, settlement, refunds, or access to Partner funds.

No database migration. Single-language mandatory.
