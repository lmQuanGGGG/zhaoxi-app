# ZhaoXi 17.6 — Unified Operations Automation & Intelligent Routing
Baseline: ZhaoXi 17.5 GREEN + READY.

Adds recommendation-only intelligent routing across the 17.5 unified operational queue. Recommendations combine SLA risk, work priority, and Admin capacity/load. Critical/overdue work receives escalation recommendations. Admin can accept or dismiss a recommendation and every decision is audited.

Guardrails: human approval required; no automatic assignment; no automatic escalation; no automatic workflow mutation; no automatic financial actions; no Partner funds authority.
