# Sprint 16.31 — Restaurant Analytics, Revenue & Promotion Performance

Cumulative release on Platform 16.30.

- Partner workspace adds a third tab: Analytics.
- Dedicated Restaurant Analytics dashboard supports 7 / 30 / 90 day periods.
- Dashboard displays revenue, order performance, promotion/coupon cost, delivery subsidy and average preparation time.
- Daily revenue trend is rendered as a compact mobile-friendly bar view.
- Top-selling menu items and coupon campaign performance are included.
- Analytics reads are authenticated and organization-scoped.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
