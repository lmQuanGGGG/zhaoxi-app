# Sprint 16.25 — Driver Dispatch, Live Tracking & Fulfillment Control

Cumulative release on Sprint 16.24.

- Driver available queue is ranked by live distance/ETA to pickup.
- Driver cannot accept a second active delivery and must be explicitly available.
- Driver Center shows dispatch distance, ETA, delivery fee and map links.
- Active jobs automatically share GPS every four seconds while permission remains granted.
- Customer tracking distinguishes pickup target vs drop-off target, shows stale GPS state and renders the persisted delivery timeline.
- Delivery-stage events are localized in Notification Center.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
