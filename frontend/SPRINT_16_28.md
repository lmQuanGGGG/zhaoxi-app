# Sprint 16.28 — Restaurant Business Hours, Auto Pause & Order Capacity Control

Cumulative release on Sprint 16.27.

- Partner Store Manager adds Restaurant Operations controls.
- Partner can enable weekly hours, pause orders, set pause reason, configure kitchen capacity and enable auto-pause.
- Kitchen counts/status refresh without overwriting unsaved Partner settings.
- Customer restaurant list, restaurant page, food service detail and checkout reflect live restaurant availability.
- Closed/paused/full restaurants keep menu visibility but disable ordering.
- Backend remains authoritative if status changes during checkout.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
