# Sprint 12.2.4 — Assignment and operations control

This integrated release adds a real dispatch workflow:

1. Admin reviews a request.
2. Admin assigns an active partner organization.
3. Partner selects its organization and sees only its assigned queue.
4. Partner accepts, processes, waits for customer, or completes the request.
5. Customer timeline receives the status history through the shared backend.

Language isolation is intentionally deferred to the final UI-polish sprint as requested.
