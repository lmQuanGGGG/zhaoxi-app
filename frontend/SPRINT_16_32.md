# Sprint 16.32 — Admin Restaurant Control & Platform Oversight

Cumulative release on Platform 16.31.

- Admin adds a dedicated Restaurants tab.
- Admin can search and filter all Food restaurants.
- Summary cards show active/suspended/platform-paused restaurants, Food orders and GMV.
- Restaurant drill-down shows Restaurant Analytics, top items, coupon performance, menu/member/capacity state.
- Admin can pause/resume new Food orders or suspend/activate a restaurant.
- Customer Food surfaces understand `platform_paused` and show the selected language only.
- Platform pause does not delete orders or Partner data.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
