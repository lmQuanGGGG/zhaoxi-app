# Sprint 16.30 — Restaurant Coupons, Campaigns & Redemption Control

Cumulative release on Platform 16.29.

- Partner Food Store Manager includes Coupon & Campaign management.
- Partner can create percent/fixed coupons with minimum order, total usage, per-Customer usage and validity window.
- Customer checkout can browse eligible coupons or enter a code manually.
- Coupon discount is displayed separately from menu promotion and delivery subsidy.
- Customer totals use: post-promotion food subtotal - coupon + gross delivery - delivery subsidy.
- Order Detail and Partner Operations preserve coupon accounting.
- Backend remains authoritative for validation and final redemption.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
