# Sprint 16.27 — Restaurant Order Management, Kitchen Queue & Partner Operations

Cumulative release on Sprint 16.26.

- Partner Restaurant Center includes a four-column Kitchen Queue.
- Kitchen cards show elapsed preparation time, late minutes, order priority and adjustable ETA.
- Partner can flag normal/high/urgent kitchen priority.
- Food sold-out/available toggle now uses an authenticated Partner endpoint.
- Existing Store Manager, external courier handoff and generic non-food Partner workflow remain compatible.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
