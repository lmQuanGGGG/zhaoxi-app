# Sprint 16.39 — Housing Viewing Appointment & Partner–Customer Follow-up

Cumulative release on Sprint 16.38 GREEN/READY.

- Customer My Rental Inquiries adds Viewing Appointment controls.
- Customer can propose a viewing time, accept Partner reschedule, cancel, and view follow-up history.
- Partner Housing Lead Pipeline can confirm, reschedule, complete or cancel a viewing.
- Both sides see the current appointment status and scheduled time.
- Confirmed appointments show a two-hour reminder marker.
- Follow-up history is visible inside each Housing lead/inquiry.
- No rental payment is introduced.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
