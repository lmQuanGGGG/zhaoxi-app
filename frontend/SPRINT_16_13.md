# Sprint 16.13 — Mobile QR Entry & Guest Bootstrap

Cumulative Platform release on Sprint 16.12.2 GREEN/READY.

- Customer and Partner public QR codes are stable app URLs, not pairing QR codes.
- Opening Customer/Partner on a phone automatically creates/restores a ZhaoXi Guest session.
- No confirmation screen and no instruction to return to another device.
- Existing 16.12.2 profile save promotes the Guest identity to persistent.
- Same browser/device can restore that ZhaoXi identity through the trusted-device credential.
- Admin login remains access-card only.
- Legacy pairing components are retained internally for future security workflows but are no longer used by the Customer/Partner gate.
