# Sprint 16.29 — Restaurant Promotions, Menu Scheduling & Discount Engine

Cumulative release on Sprint 16.28.

- Partner food menu cards include Promotion & Schedule controls.
- Promotions support percent-off, fixed promotional price and quantity bundle pricing.
- Promotions can be restricted by date, time, weekdays and minimum quantity.
- A separate menu sale schedule can hide ordering outside the configured sale window.
- Customer food listing, restaurant detail, food service detail and checkout use the live pricing endpoint.
- Checkout displays original food amount, promotion discount and final food subtotal before delivery pricing.
- Order Detail preserves promotion accounting.
- Backend remains authoritative for final order price.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
