# Sprint 16.38 — Housing Search, Availability & Lead Management

Cumulative release on Sprint 16.37 GREEN/READY.

- Housing search adds price, move-in, availability and sort controls.
- Housing cards show Available / Reserved / Rented in the selected language.
- Partner can set Housing availability for new and existing listings.
- Housing detail blocks new rental inquiries when a listing is Reserved or Rented.
- Partner Operations adds a six-stage Housing Lead Pipeline.
- Customer adds My Rental Inquiries tracking.
- No rental payment is introduced.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
