# Sprint 16.26 — Partner Restaurant Fulfillment & External Courier Handoff

Cumulative release on Sprint 16.25.1.

- Partner Restaurant Center now manages preparation and external courier handoff.
- External food workflow: accept -> preparing -> ready for pickup -> courier booked -> handed off -> delivered.
- Partner can record courier/provider, phone and delivery reference.
- Customer order progress reflects the same external fulfillment stages.
- Non-food Partner services keep the legacy generic status workflow.
- Partner order reads/writes forward authenticated ZhaoXi session credentials.
- Single-language remains mandatory.
